How a brief runs

From question to evidence.

Frame

  • Name the decision
  • Name the people to hear from
  • Fix the date the answer is needed

Design

  • Questionnaire, sample and method written down
  • Where it will sit beside observed behaviour
  • What it will not be able to say

Field

  • Collect the answers
  • Check them for consistency
  • Keep the method with the result

Report

  • Findings first, tables next, method last
  • Confidence stated on every figure
  • A branded PDF, or a live screen
What it adds

Behaviour tells you what. Answers tell you why.

Observed

  • What people watched
  • When, and how much
  • Measured from public platform data

Asked

  • What people say they want
  • What they think of a brand or category
  • Collected directly from respondents

Together

  • Behaviour checked against belief
  • Gaps named, not smoothed over
  • A decision with both behind it
Questions, answered

Straight answers.

Why pair surveys with platform data?

Platform data shows what happened. Only a question can ask why. The two answer different things, and a decision is stronger with both.

Who sees the results?

You do, in the form you agree up front. Our standing rule for audience data is percentages, never individuals.

What about the academic network?

The institutions are listed as an academic and mentor network and are to be mentioned only in the relationship form actually authorised.
