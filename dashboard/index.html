Method

From a live stream to a decision.

Eight steps, and none of them is skipped. A finding is never an opinion: it is a rule applied to measured numbers, and it arrives with its evidence and a confidence.

  1. 01
    CollectPublic YouTube data, read every few minutes, live streams every thirty seconds.
  2. 02
    VerifyIntegrity checks, then the radar: unusual patterns are flagged, never hidden.
  3. 03
    MeasureWeekly views, prime time, STX and the IFX Score turn raw counts into measures.
  4. 04
    FindRules over the measures produce findings, each with numbers and a confidence.
  5. 05
    BriefThe same findings, written for a buyer, a broadcaster, a campaign or a creator.
  6. 06
    ReportA branded PDF: findings first, then tables, then how it was worked out.
  7. 07
    ActPlan the spend, choose the messenger, decide the hour.
  8. 08
    LearnA reviewer’s decision on a flagged channel changes what later reports say.

Trust

Built to be checked, not just believed.

Media numbers are only worth paying for if you can defend them in a boardroom. These are the rules every StatAxis figure is built under.

P1

Show the working

Every finding carries its numbers and a confidence. No number arrives without its evidence.

P2

Missing is not zero

If we did not measure it, we say so. We never fill a gap with a guess.

P3

Flags are not verdicts

Unusual patterns go to a person to review. We say “check”, never “fake”.

P4

Audience needs a source

State and age appear only with a source and a date. Otherwise: not known.

P5

Reports that stay ours

Branded PDFs made for you, readable by people, not by scripts.

P6

Honest about the edges

YouTube only, today. Other platforms are on the roadmap, not in the numbers.

What we measure

  • Concurrent viewers of live streams, every thirty seconds
  • Views, likes and comments of every new upload
  • Subscribers and their change over time
  • Share of attention by group, language and hour

What we never claim

  • Who exactly watched, or why they watched
  • An audience’s age or place that nobody supplied
  • That an unusual pattern is proof of wrongdoing
  • Saves, shares or conversions, which YouTube does not publish
