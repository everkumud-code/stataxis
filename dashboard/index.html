Trust

Built to be checked.

Six rules behind every figure.

P1

Show the working

Numbers and confidence on every finding.

P2

Missing is not zero

Unmeasured is stated, never guessed.

P3

Flags are not verdicts

A person reviews. We say “check”.

P4

Audience needs a source

State and age only with source and date.

P5

Reports stay ours

Branded PDFs, readable by people, not scripts.

P6

Honest edges

YouTube today. Instagram and Facebook from Jan 2027, X in 2027, CTV from Jul 2027.

We measure

  • Live viewers, every minute
  • Views, likes and comments of new uploads
  • Subscribers and their change

We never claim

  • Who watched, or why
  • An audience nobody supplied
  • Saves, shares or conversions
Coverage

Where the signal goes next.

YouTube is live. The rest follows in this order, as each platform’s data access allows.

  1. NowYouTube

    Live viewers every minute, uploads, views, likes, comments and subscribers for the channels we track.

  2. Jan 2027Instagram and Facebook

    Planned. Public account metrics and, where an account connects, its own audience, within what each platform’s API allows.

  3. 2027X

    Planned for 2027, subject to the platform’s data access terms.

  4. Jul 2027Connected TV

    Planned. CTV intelligence alongside digital video, with the same rule: show the source, show the confidence.
