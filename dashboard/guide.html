STATAXIS USER GUIDE

How to Use StatAxis

Learn how to read channel data, choose a time interval, compare channels, evaluate signals and understand the STX Index™.

Public preview: the dashboard displays DEMO DATA. Live StatAxis data and tools require an approved profile.

01 · Request Access

1
Select Request Access on the dashboard.
2
Submit your name, organization, custom-domain email, mobile number, purpose of use and requested package.
3
Your application is reviewed by StatAxis administration. Access starts only after approval.

02 · Explore Channel Data

Channel-level data is the primary StatAxis view. Select a market tab:

Hindi · English India · English World · Regional India · Regional World

Inside each market, channels remain individual records. Market totals, averages and peaks are supporting summaries.

03 · Show & Time Interval

Show

Choose what you want to inspect: individual channel audience, peak concurrent audience, average concurrent audience, view movement or intelligence.

Time Interval

Select the interval between a start and end time—for example 09:00:00 to 10:00:00. The timeline can show observations at their recorded timestamps, such as 09:02:05.

04 · Compare Channels

Comparison is built around individual channels. Select A vs B vs C and optionally the Average reference.

Example: Aaj Tak vs IndiaTV vs The Lallantop vs Average.

The average is a reference; it does not replace the individual channel records.

05 · Understand STX Index™

STX Index™ is StatAxis's composite intelligence indicator. It provides a quick 0–100 view of available measured signals.

Read it together with Confidence, Signal Drivers, underlying channel data and historical movement.

06 · Evaluate

Approved users can submit a YouTube URL and assign it a custom name.

The result presents the available observations, STX Index™, confidence and:

DATA → ANALYSIS → STATAXIS VIEW

07 · Methodology

StatAxis preserves timestamped observations and keeps channel records separate. Historical views use observations actually available; missing history is not silently invented.

Totals, averages and peaks are supporting analytical views. Channel-level observations remain the primary record.

StatAxis separates measured information from interpretation and does not present unsupported causality as fact.
