02Honesty

A score that knows how sure it is.

  • Ranked against peers, not a fixed scale
  • Unmeasurable parts are left out; the rest is re-weighted

Not measured

  • Other platforms (YouTube only, for now)
  • Why anyone watched, or who they are

The twelve parts

What each part looks at.

Impact

  • Depth of reaction: likes plus five times the comments, per view
  • Conversation: comments per 1,000 views
  • Staying power: videos older than ten days still gaining views

Followership

  • Audience size
  • Loyalty: a mature upload’s median views as a share of subscribers
  • Growth, once three days of history exist
  • Live turnout: peak viewers at the creator’s own live streams, against subscribers (only for creators who went live)

eXposure

  • Reach per upload: the median video
  • Regular publishing: uploads per week and how even the gaps are
  • Steady results: how little views swing between uploads
  • Hit rate: how often an upload reaches the typical peer upload
  • Fresh uploads: how recently the creator published
Questions, answered

Straight answers.

What does preliminary mean?

The score rests on little evidence: few uploads, no subscriber data or short history. It is pulled toward 50 and tagged so it cannot look sure.

Why only YouTube?

That is where public data exists today. Instagram and Facebook are planned for January 2027, X for 2027, connected TV by July 2027.

What does a radar flag do?

An open high-risk flag takes 10 points. A flag a reviewer confirms withholds the score. A cleared flag stops counting.

Steady, Rising, Hit-driven?

Plain-words profiles beside the number: Steady results, a Rising audience, Hit-driven reach, or Impact-, Follower- or Exposure-led when one pillar leads.
