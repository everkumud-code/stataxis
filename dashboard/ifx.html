01Three pillars

Why three letters.

A creator can be loved and small, or huge and ignored, or everywhere and forgotten. One number hides which. Three pillars show it.

IImpact · 35%

How strongly the content lands.

Depth of reaction (likes and comments per view) and staying power: do videos older than ten days keep earning views? Impact is what people do after they watch.

Depth of reactionStaying power
FFollowership · 35%

The audience behind the creator.

Its size, its loyalty (how many of the subscribers turn up for a typical upload) and its growth over the last days and weeks.

Audience sizeViews per subscriberGrowth
XeXposure · 30%

How far and how often they are seen.

Reach per upload (the median video), regular publishing (uploads per week and how even the gaps are) and steady results (how little views swing from one upload to the next).

02Honesty

A score that knows how sure it is.

Built in

  • Each part is ranked against the other creators in the same comparison, not against a fixed scale
  • A part that cannot be measured is left out, and the rest is re-weighted
  • Few uploads or little history pulls the shown score toward 50
  • Open high-risk flags from the authenticity radar take 10 points; a confirmed flag withholds the score
  • Profile in plain words: Steady, Rising, Hit-driven, Impact-led, Follower-led, Exposure-led, Balanced

Not measured

  • Saves, shares and conversions: YouTube does not publish them for other people's videos
  • Exposure on other platforms: this score is YouTube only for now
  • Why anyone watched, or who they are
