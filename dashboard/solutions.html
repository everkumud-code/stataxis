Questions, answered

Straight answers.

How do we start?

Tell us the decision, the audience, the channels and the date. We write the question back in a form the data can answer.

What will you tell me you cannot measure?

Saves, shares and conversions, anything outside YouTube today, and who exactly watched. We say so in the brief before work starts.

Can the work be recurring?

Yes. A weekly briefing, a live screen for an event, or an API feeding your own dashboards.

Is the audience data verified?

Audience profiles are shown only where a channel or a named study supplied them, with the source and date. Channels can join the verified audience programme to supply theirs.
