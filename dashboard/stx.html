How to read it

Three habits before you quote a score.

Read the confidence first

  • A high score on thin evidence is tagged preliminary
  • Confidence shows how much of the evidence exists
  • Quote the two together

Compare like with like

  • Same period, same group
  • A score is relative to the comparison
  • Say which comparison you used

Ask what moved

  • Growth, momentum and acceleration show direction
  • Consistency shows how steady it is
  • Anomaly marks unusual movement, not a verdict
Questions, answered

Straight answers.

Is STX a raw audience number?

No. It combines audience, growth, momentum, acceleration, consistency, competition and anomaly evidence into one qualified view.

What if a part cannot be measured?

It is shown as unavailable and never silently counted as poor performance. Missing is not zero.

Does STX explain why a channel moved?

No. STX qualifies signals. It does not invent causes that the evidence does not support.

Where is STX shown?

On channel views, in rankings and on the live compare screen, always with its confidence and period.
