Proprietary intelligence

STX Index™ turns many signals into one qualified view.

STX is not a raw audience number. It combines audience, growth, momentum, acceleration, consistency, competition and anomaly or event evidence, with confidence travelling alongside the score.

01Methodology

Seven weighted components.

02Principles

Three promises the score keeps.

01

Confidence travels with the score

Evidence coverage matters. A score built on thin data is pulled toward the middle, and says so.

02

Missing is not zero

Unavailable evidence is never silently treated as poor performance.

03

No invented causality

STX qualifies signals. It does not manufacture explanations the evidence cannot support.
