The platform

One platform. Three layers. No guesswork.

StatAxis turns timestamped observations of Indian news video into comparable measurement, and then into findings you can act on. Here is what is inside.

01Architecture

Data. Analysis. The StatAxis view.

Every answer on the platform passes through the same three layers, and you can see all of them.

DATA

What was observed

Every figure keeps the moment it was measured and the source it came from. A gap stays a gap. It is never quietly filled.

ANALYSIS

What changed

Comparable observations, set against the competition: share, movement, rank, peaks, steadiness.

VIEW

What it means

The StatAxis view says what the evidence supports, in plain words. It never invents a cause the data cannot show.

02Instruments

Everything you can do with it.

Briefing

Findings for your role

The week's findings, written for a media buyer, a broadcaster, a campaign, a creator or an audience member, with the evidence attached.

Live

Prime time and live compare

Average and peak audience for every evening hour, and up to thirty channels on one live screen with STX and the tone of what each shows.

Reports

Weekly views and the report builder

Pick the days, the hours, live or videos or Shorts, the feed and the genre. Download a branded PDF in one click.

Creators

IFX Score

Impact, Followership, eXposure for people who publish instead of broadcast, ranked against their true peers.

Spend

Media planner

Cost per thousand live viewers from your own rates, a value index and a suggested budget split, narrowed to your target.

Trust

Authenticity radar

Six signals of unusual viewership, shown with their evidence and kept with the reviewer's decision.

Audience

Place and age, where known

State, age group and gender, only where a channel or a study supplied them, always with source and date.

Market

Market, channel, content, competition

The foundation: Hindi, English, business and regional markets; a channel in depth; content velocity; side-by-side competition.

Control

People and access

Package access, per-person privileges, seat limits, and machine-readable exports as a privilege of their own.

03Filters

One way to ask the question.

The same six filters work across the platform, so a question you learn to ask once can be asked anywhere.

Filter by

  • Category: news, business, print, parties, leaders, commentators, creators, journalists
  • Language: Hindi, English, regional
  • Time: the dates, and the hours of the day
  • Place and age group, where the audience is known
  • An individual channel, or a handful

And it will tell you when

  • A channel's audience is not known, instead of guessing
  • A figure rests on thin data, with a confidence beside it
  • A video was not measured close enough to count
  • A flag has been cleared or confirmed by a reviewer
