01 / STATAXIS

What is StatAxis?

StatAxis is built around a simple proposition: digital media measurement should not stop at a number. The system preserves timestamped observations, separates measurement types, compares channels and markets across time, and converts supported signals into a structured intelligence layer.

From measurement to intelligence

Audience, views, concurrency, velocity, engagement, rank, share and movement are treated as distinct evidence. Correlation is not presented as automatic causation.

A market-level system

The hierarchy spans Market → Period → Channel → Stream → Video → Competition, allowing channel and market questions to be answered from the same evidence base.

Evidence has a memory

Measurements retain observation time and provenance. Missing history is not silently reconstructed, and missing evidence is not automatically turned into zero.

Built for comparison

Primary versus all streams, current versus historical periods, channel versus channel and market versus market can be examined in one workflow.

03 / THE GAME-CHANGER LAYER

STX Index™ — one score, many signals

STX Index is a composite intelligence score. It is not a raw audience number, a simple popularity rank, or a substitute for the underlying data. It summarizes multiple dimensions of measurable performance into a single qualified 0–100 signal while preserving its components and confidence.

Why a composite index?

A channel can have large audience scale but weak growth; another can have smaller scale but rapid momentum. STX keeps these dimensions visible while producing a common signal.

Why confidence matters

An STX score without evidence quality can create false precision. Confidence travels alongside the score, and sparse evidence should reduce qualification rather than be disguised as certainty.

What STX is not

STX is not a raw view count, guaranteed prediction, causal attribution engine, or replacement for the underlying observations. It is a qualified intelligence signal.

Supporting signals

Signals such as view velocity can help explain movement and feed the intelligence layer even when they are not one of the seven published weighted components of STX v0.
