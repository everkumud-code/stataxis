The audience YouTube will not show you, and how to buy responsibly anyway

No public tool can tell you the age or state of someone else's viewers. Here is what you can do instead.

StatAxis · 2026-10-10 · 6 min read

Sooner or later every media buyer asks the same question: will this reach 25 to 34 year olds in Bihar? It is the right question. Unfortunately, it is the one question YouTube's public data cannot answer.

What is not public

YouTube shows a channel's owner the age, gender and location of their viewers inside their own analytics. It does not publish them for anyone else's channel, and the public interface that measurement companies use has no such field. Any tool that shows you a confident age split for a channel it does not own is estimating, and often guessing.

What StatAxis does about it

It does not guess. Instead, it keeps a place for the real thing. When a channel shares its audience, from its own YouTube Studio screens or its media kit, or when a named study supplies one, StatAxis stores it with the source and the date: age bands, gender, states, countries. Every screen that shows it shows where it came from. Every other channel says plainly: audience not known.

Where we know, we show the source. Where we do not, we say so.

Using it in a plan

In the planner you can choose a target: age groups, states, a gender. For every channel with a profile, StatAxis works out the share of its audience inside your target, and multiplies the channel's average audience by it. The result is the matched audience, and the cost is shown per 1,000 matched viewers. Channels with no profile are listed as no audience data and are left out of the budget split, not scored with a made-up share.

A caveat we state in the method: a profile gives age, state and gender separately, so a combined target multiplies them as if they were independent. That is an approximation, and it is labelled as one.

The verified audience programme

The gap closes fastest when channels choose to close it. If you run a channel or a creator brand, you can share your split, in percentages and never individual viewers, and we will publish it with your name, the source and the date. You can update or withdraw it whenever you like. Buyers get something they can plan against; you get a verified mark in every plan that cares where people live.

The rule

Buy against an audience you can name the source of. If the only source is a tool's confidence, you are not buying data. You are buying a tone of voice.
